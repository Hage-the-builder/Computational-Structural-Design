# Computational Structural Design

A personal engineering project combining physical construction, Python-based structural analysis, and CAD modeling.

## Overview

I designed and built a backyard calisthenics gym on a budget of approximately $150. Alongside the physical build, I began developing computational tools and a CAD model to better understand the structural behavior of the design.

The project grew from a practical question: how can I design a useful structure on a limited budget while accounting for the loads and forces it may experience?

## Engineering Analysis

I used Python to explore structural calculations relevant to the design, including:

- **Euler buckling:** examining the stability of compression members.
- **Bearing stress:** analyzing contact forces between structural components.
- **Dynamic loading:** exploring how moving or suddenly applied loads differ from static loads.
- **Work-energy principle:** applying energy relationships to estimate the effects of dynamic loading.

These calculations helped connect mathematical and physical principles to an actual engineering problem.

## CAD Modeling

I am developing a CAD model of the gym to represent its components and dimensions.

**Current status:** Component modeling and dimensions are complete; final assembly is in progress.

The goal is to create a digital representation of the physical structure and improve how I visualize its geometry and arrangement.

## Project Goals

- Apply mathematical and physical principles to a real construction problem.
- Use Python to organize and perform engineering calculations.
- Develop CAD skills through dimensioned component modeling and assembly.
- Explore how computational tools can support early-stage engineering design.

## Related Project

I also developed **Gym Structural Analyzer (GSA)**, a public Python package for structural analysis that grew out of this project.

## Scope and Limitations

This is an independent educational project, not a professionally certified engineering design. Calculations depend on their assumptions and inputs and do not, by themselves, establish that a structure is safe for use. The project is an opportunity to learn, test ideas, and improve my engineering and programming skills.
